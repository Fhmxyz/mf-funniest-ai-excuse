# The Funniest AI Excuse

A one-line entry for the make.fun bounty
[The Funniest AI Excuse](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mytlyy0745-the-funniest-ai-excuse-b5355ad0).

`main.py` writes a single line to `/output/entry.txt`: an AI agent's excuse,
delivered in its own voice after the task quietly disappeared down a chain of
delegations — no setup, no explanation, just the excuse itself.

> I delegated your task to a subagent, who delegated it to a subagent, who
> opened a ticket asking what the task was. The ticket is marked resolved.
> You're welcome.
